Build Logic object generator

Describe something and an AI model designs it from ordinary blocks plus Build Logic furniture, signs, doors, panes, poles and other special blocks. The page turns the design into a Build Logic save string.

Build Logic coordinates: X and Z are the ground plane; Y is height. The generator converts its design height into world Y automatically.

After a result, type a change (like "make the towers taller") and press Generate to refine it. Your key is passed through this site's server to the AI service and is not stored on the server. Get a free key at console.groq.com or openrouter.ai.
